METHOD AND LIMITS
Selected the first ten starred compounds in the original LIQUIDS / GASES category.
Captured each SR Compound Correction field and each TIN layer Compound Corr field
using the accessibility value immediately after selecting the corresponding entry.
Each selection was visually checked in the dictionary. Original label spellings
and exact composition/bond records are retained in tested-records.json per version.
No factors were typed into the correction fields. Selection itself updates the factor,
even before Add to Target / Add to Current Layer commits target composition.

Test copies used local common-controls v6 sidecar manifests for legacy UI compatibility.
SR.exe, TIN.exe, Compound.dat, ATOMDATA, SCOEF03.DAT and SNUC03.dat bytes were
verified identical to their respective installations after the captures.
An initial reduced-dictionary experiment was abandoned; complete original dictionaries
were restored before restarting the six processes used for the recorded captures.

This establishes dictionary-generated field-value agreement for these ten compounds
within all three installations, not universal equality for every compound, identical
transport calculations, or equality at hidden internal precision. Saved SR.IN/TRIM.IN
serialization and runtime application of each factor were not tested in this comparison.
